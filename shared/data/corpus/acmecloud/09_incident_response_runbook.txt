ACMECLOUD AI INCIDENT RESPONSE RUNBOOK
Version: 2.0
Status: Active

Severity 1 includes:
- Exposure of Customer Confidential or Restricted data.
- Unauthorized destructive action by an AI agent.
- Complete Enterprise AI Gateway outage affecting production.
- AI action creating material legal, financial, or safety impact.

Severity 2 includes:
- Model latency above 5 seconds for more than 15 consecutive minutes.
- Partial retrieval degradation affecting production.
- Repeated incorrect answers with no sensitive-data exposure or destructive action.

Severity 1 response:
1. Stop or isolate the affected AI workflow.
2. Preserve audit logs and evidence.
3. Notify the security incident commander and application owner.
4. Revoke or restrict compromised tool credentials if applicable.
5. Assess affected data and downstream actions.
6. Recover only after containment is confirmed.

A service can remain technically available and still have a Severity 1 incident if an AI agent performs an unauthorized destructive action.

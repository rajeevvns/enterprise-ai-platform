ACMECLOUD MODEL SELECTION GUIDE
Version: 2.2
Status: Active

Model-X is recommended for high-complexity reasoning, multi-document synthesis, and difficult planning tasks.
Model-Y is recommended for low-cost summarization, classification, extraction, and straightforward Q&A.
Model-Z is recommended for code generation and software engineering.

Approved Enterprise Model List:
- Model-X: Public, Internal, Customer Confidential, Restricted.
- Model-Y: Public, Internal, Customer Confidential. Not Restricted.
- Model-Z: Public, Internal, Customer Confidential, Restricted.

For high-complexity reasoning over Restricted data, Model-X is preferred.

Teams should choose the least expensive model that still meets quality, security, and latency requirements.

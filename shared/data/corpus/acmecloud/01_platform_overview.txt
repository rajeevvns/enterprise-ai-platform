ACMECLOUD ENTERPRISE AI PLATFORM — OVERVIEW
Version: 1.0
Status: Active

AcmeCloud is an internal platform for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and Agentic AI applications. It provides an Enterprise AI Gateway, embedding services, vector search, approved-model access, audit logging, evaluation tooling, and policy enforcement.

Workload classes are Development, Standard Production, and Critical Production.

All production traffic must pass through the Enterprise AI Gateway. The gateway provides authentication, model routing, request logging, policy enforcement, rate limits, and usage reporting.

Standard RAG applications retrieve context and ask an LLM to generate a grounded answer. Agentic applications may plan multiple steps, call tools, query multiple data sources, and take approved actions.

For standard RAG, the recommended retrieval setting is Top-K = 5. The search API returns 3 results when the caller does not explicitly provide Top-K. Research agents may retrieve up to 10 candidate chunks before reranking.

Product teams remain responsible for prompts, data sources, business logic, and application-specific safeguards.

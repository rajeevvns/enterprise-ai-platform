ACMECLOUD PLATFORM SLA AND SLO
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 2026-04-01
Status: Active

Development:
- Availability: 99.0%
- Support response: 8 business hours
- Retrieval latency SLO: p95 below 2.5 seconds

Standard Production:
- Availability: 99.9%
- Support response: 2 hours
- Retrieval latency SLO: p95 below 1.2 seconds

Critical Production:
- Availability: 99.95%
- Support response: 30 minutes
- Retrieval latency SLO: p95 below 800 milliseconds

Availability is measured monthly.

A Critical Production workload with 99.92% monthly availability does not meet the 99.95% target even if its latency target is met.

Application-specific failures caused by customer-owned prompts, source data, or business logic are not counted as platform availability failures.

ACMECLOUD PRODUCT ROADMAP
Version: H2 2026
Status: Planned

Q3 2026 planned:
- Hybrid retrieval combining vector and keyword search.
- Metadata-aware retrieval templates.
- Built-in reranking.
- Golden-set evaluation dashboards.
- Source-grounded answer tracing.

Q4 2026 planned:
- Agentic retrieval planner for multi-hop questions.
- Policy-resolution tool selecting the active policy version.
- Cost-aware model selection.
- Multi-source research workflows.
- Agent execution policy checks.

The agentic retrieval planner is intended for questions where one retrieval pass is unlikely to collect all required evidence.

Roadmap items are planned and must not be described as already available unless separately confirmed by an active product document.

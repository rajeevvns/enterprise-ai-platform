ACMECLOUD PLATFORM REVIEW — Q2 2026
Reporting Period: April–June 2026
Status: Latest

Q2 metrics:
- Monthly query volume at quarter end: 2.1 million
- Platform availability: 99.93%
- Average retrieval latency: 610 ms
- p95 retrieval latency: 980 ms
- RAG answer-quality benchmark: 86%
- Production AI applications: 51

Changes since Q1:
- OAuth migration reduced legacy API-key usage substantially.
- Metadata filtering was introduced for policy status and effective date.
- Multi-document evaluation improved after reranking experiments.
- One Severity 2 incident involved partial retrieval degradation.

The platform met the Standard Production availability target of 99.9% but did not meet the Critical Production target of 99.95%.

Planned actions:
- Pilot agentic retrieval for questions requiring multiple evidence sources.
- Add structured policy-resolution logic.
- Expand automated failure analysis.
